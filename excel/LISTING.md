# Listing: Off-Grid Solar Sizing Workbook (Excel)

## Sales blurb (about 150 words)

Size an off-grid solar system in minutes and show your working. The Off-Grid Solar Sizing Workbook is a formula-driven Excel file (no macros, Excel 2016+) that turns a simple load list into a complete first-pass design: daily energy, 12/24/48 V system voltage, panel count, battery bank layout (series x parallel, Ah and usable Wh), inverter rating checked against both continuous load and motor start-up surge, MPPT controller rating, DC fuse sizes, copper cable sizes checked for voltage drop and fuse ampacity, and the AC breaker.

Every intermediate number is visible, and a one-page Report sheet writes out each formula step with the actual values so a client, installer or reviewer can audit it line by line. Defaults cover LiFePO4 and lead-acid; standard-size tables are editable for your market.

The formulas mirror an open, unit-tested sizing engine and were verified inside Excel against hand-calculated examples. Built by Keshav Jha. Preliminary design aid, not a stamped design.
